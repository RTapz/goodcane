# Flavour bottle images

- Made by `scripts/assets/make_bottles.py` from one photo, `scripts/assets/bottle-source.jpg`: a glass bottle of juice among sliced fruit, cut out along a hand-traced outline and recoloured once per flavour accent (`app/utils/flavour-colours.ts`). The white cap and glass highlights keep their own colour.
- The bottle is a generic product photo, not GoodCane's packaging or artwork (spec constraint 6). It stands in for "a bottle of this flavour" in a demo where everything is made up.
