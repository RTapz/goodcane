# Flavour bottle images

- Made by `scripts/assets/make_bottles.py` from one photo, `scripts/assets/bottle-source.jpg`: cut out, smoothed, given a gentle cylinder shading and recoloured once per flavour accent (`app/utils/flavour-colours.ts`).
- The bottle is a generic product photo, not GoodCane's packaging or artwork (spec constraint 6). It stands in for "a bottle of this flavour" in a demo where everything is made up.
- Photo source and licence: photo by Joan Tran on Unsplash, used under the Unsplash Licence (free to use, attribution appreciated).
